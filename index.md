Welcome to my portfolio. My work focuses on applying data analytics to sports performance, especially in tennis.

## Featured Projects

### WTA Tennis Professional Analytics
A multi-part analysis of WTA match data from 2020–2024, examining winner-loser differences, top-player ranking trajectories, tournament planning patterns, and serve metrics associated with ranking improvement.  
[Read project](./wta-tennis-professional-analytics.md)

### Is Height Associated with First Serve Speed?
A tennis performance project exploring the relationship between player height and first-serve speed.  
[Read project](./height-first-serve-speed.md)

### WTA Top 100 vs WNBA: How Different Are Athlete Profiles?
A cross-sport comparison project analyzing athlete profile differences between elite women’s tennis and basketball.  
[Read project](./wta-top-100-vs-wnba-body-profile.md)

### Las Vegas Aces Player Load Dashboard

An interactive Streamlit dashboard built with Kinexon tracking data to monitor player workload, visualize load trends, and support individual and team-level performance analysis. The app includes load summaries, acute and chronic load views, and team reporting tools.

[Open dashboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/XinyueCindy/Las-Vegas-Aces-Players-Load-Kinexon)
